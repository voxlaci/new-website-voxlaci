A complete STELLA weekend

Experience the festival from within.

The STELLA Individual Residence is designed for singers, conductors, music teachers and other individual participants who would like to experience STELLA throughout the weekend without travelling as part of a choir.

Stay in Cascais, take part in the festival atmosphere, attend concerts and workshops, meet other singers and conductors, and enjoy the STELLA Christmas Market.
