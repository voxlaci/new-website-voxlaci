Who operates this website.

VoxLaci is the public and artistic activity of Associação Vox SDR. This page gathers the essential institutional information for visitors, families, partners and validation teams.

Official statement

Associação Vox SDR owns and operates voxlaci.com.

Associação Vox SDR owns and operates voxlaci.com. The domain, content, forms, registrations, contacts and institutional communications published on voxlaci.com are managed by Associação Vox SDR, the entity responsible for VoxLaci activity.

The website exists to inform the public about choirs, ensembles, educational projects, cultural events, musical services, registrations, payments, contacts and VoxLaci community initiatives in Cascais, Portugal.

Transparency and content

Clear information for visitors and reviewers.

Mission

To promote choral practice, music education, artistic participation and community through choirs, ensembles, projects, festivals, training and cultural services.

Activity

VoxLaci works with children, young people and adults, organises musical events, hosts international projects and provides artistic and educational services.

Editorial responsibility

Content published on voxlaci.com is prepared and updated by the VoxLaci / Associação Vox SDR team. Corrections may be requested by email.
