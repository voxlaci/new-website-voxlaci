1/5 · Choose the modality
Extra Night · Sunday to Monday

With the Choral Residence, you can extend your stay for one additional night. Double/twin/triple room: €95 per person with breakfast and Sunday dinner, or €90 without dinner. Single room: €125 with dinner, or €120 without dinner. You will select this option in the rooming list before payment, and it will be added automatically to the total. No other extra nights are available, either before or after the festival.

Participating on more than one day

Choirs may participate in one or more STELLA Choir Days. Registration is made separately for each day of participation. If a choir wishes to attend on two different days, both Choir Days must be selected and registered separately.

Example: Saturday + Sunday = 2 Choir Days. Each Choir Day gives access to the programme and experiences scheduled for that particular day.
