Why this name?

A wave starts at one point. Then it travels through everything.

Kýma — κύμα — means “wave” in Greek, representing the propagation of sound, emotion and ideas: like a wave that reaches and transforms whoever receives it. That is the spirit of the eight singers who make up KYMA: soloists who each take on the most demanding parts individually, and who together build a cohesive vocal core. KYMA's first project is Mozart's Requiem, in Cascais, in November 2026. The sound fades. The impact can carry on.

Casting

Open casting for 8 soloists.

We are looking for singers with:

  • Choral and/or solo experience
  • Solid vocal technique
  • Music reading skills — using a tablet with a sheet-music app (ForScore, Mobile Sheets or similar) is a plus
  • Autonomy preparing scores
  • Availability to prepare and sing every solo in Mozart’s Requiem, as well as the choral parts
  • Playing an instrument is a plus
  • Availability for all rehearsals and the production
  • Punctuality and attendance
  • Ability to integrate into a small, high-level vocal group, with a taste for team spirit and commitment to the project

Preference will be given to professional singers/soloists, or those aspiring to be.

In your application, include a brief description of your musical background and the solos you have performed.

Commitment to rehearsals and team spirit: vocal warm-up is led, on rotation, by every member of the group.

Paid rehearsals and concerts — reference rates: €20/rehearsal and €70/concert, to be confirmed at the casting meeting.
