Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

How does the hire work?

Prices apply to each 24-hour period. Equipment must be collected and returned by the customer at the Torre d'Águilha reception in São Domingos de Rana, open all day. Confirm availability and terms by email or WhatsApp.

What equipment is available?

Quick sound system (two speakers, mixing desk and microphones): €180. Professional wireless microphones: €65 each. Party lighting system: €120. Smoke machines: €55 each. Seven-seat electric van or nine-seat diesel van, subject to availability: €100 per day.

Is a security deposit required?

Yes. The security deposit is four times the hire price of the equipment. It is paid at collection and returned after the equipment is delivered back, subject to the hire terms.

How can I make a booking?

By email at info@voxlaci.com or WhatsApp at +351 925 075 186. Please state your dates and required equipment — we reply within 24 hours.
