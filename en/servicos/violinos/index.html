Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Who teaches the violin lessons?

Paula Fernandes holds a degree in Musicology from FCSH/UNL, a Higher Diploma in Violin and a General Diploma in Viola, and teaches in specialised artistic music education.

How do group lessons work?

They last 30 minutes, cost €15 per lesson and per student, and operate with a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 10 students. A group opens with at least 2 enrolments and may welcome new students up to 10, subject to availability.

How are individual lessons priced?

The price is always €1 per minute. The student chooses the preferred duration, subject to availability: for example, 30 minutes cost €30, 45 minutes cost €45 and 60 minutes cost €60. These are examples, not mandatory packages.

How much is paid each month?

You pay only for lessons actually scheduled that month. Four group lessons cost, for example, €60 per student; three lessons cost €45. This is not a fixed monthly fee independent of the calendar.

Are there lessons during school holidays?

No. There are no lessons during school holidays, and those weeks are not charged.

What happens if a student misses a group lesson?

The scheduled lesson is charged because it takes place with the other participants and the student's place remains reserved. An individual absence does not qualify for a make-up lesson, discount or refund.

How does cancellation of an individual lesson work?

If you cannot attend, give at least one week's notice and the lesson will not be charged. This allows the teacher to reorganise the timetable and make that period available. With less than one week's notice, or in the event of a no-show, the lesson is charged.

Can I change between group and individual lessons?

Yes. You may change from one month to the next, subject to timetable availability and, for group lessons, an available place.

How much is the trial lesson?

The individual trial lesson lasts 20 minutes and costs €30.

Are there group violin workshops?

Yes. Group workshops cost €25, have a maximum of 8 students and will be announced on VoxLaci social media.

Do I need to own a violin?

You do not need an instrument to start. If you own a violin, you may bring it. Contact us to discuss available options.

Is school transport available?

Transport from school to Torre d'Águilha may be available between 4:30pm and 5:30pm, with a reference price of €10 per day. See the transport page for details.

Where do lessons take place?

At Seminário Torre d'Águilha in São Domingos de Rana, Cascais.
