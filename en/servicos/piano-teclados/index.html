Formats and prices

Learn piano and keyboard at your own pace.

Choose regular individual lessons, group lessons or a trial lesson.

Individual lessons — €1 per minute

Individual tuition on a day and at a time to be arranged. Choose the duration you prefer, subject to availability. The following prices are examples:

  • 30 minutes — €30 per lesson
  • 45 minutes — €45 per lesson
  • 60 minutes — €60 per lesson

If you cannot attend, notify us at least one week in advance and the lesson will not be charged. With less than one week’s notice, or in case of absence without notice, the lesson is charged.

Group lessons — 2 to 10 students

30 minutes · €15 per lesson/student. The group opens when there are at least 2 students and can welcome new participants up to a maximum of 10, subject to availability.

If a student is absent, the lesson continues with the remaining participants. The missed lesson is not rescheduled, discounted or refunded because the place remains reserved.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Which instruments can I learn?

You can learn piano and keyboard through individual or group lessons, subject to the selected format and availability.

On which days do lessons take place?

Lessons take place on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, at a day and time to be arranged, subject to availability.

What lesson formats are available?

Choose regular individual lessons at €1 per minute or 30-minute group lessons with 2 to 10 students at €15 per lesson per student.

Are lessons available in English?

Yes. Piano & Keyboards da Águilha lessons are bilingual — Portuguese and English — and suitable for international families and English-speaking residents in the Cascais area.

How much do individual lessons cost?

Regular individual lessons cost €1 per minute and students choose the duration, subject to availability. For example: 30 minutes costs €30, 45 minutes costs €45 and 60 minutes costs €60. The one-off private lesson costs €60 for 30 minutes and the trial lesson costs €30 for 20 minutes.

How much is paid each month?

You pay only for the lessons scheduled that month. It is not a fixed monthly fee independent of the calendar. There are no lessons during school holidays, and those weeks are not charged.

Can I change between group and individual lessons?

Yes. You can change format from one month to the next, subject to timetable availability and, for group lessons, an available place.

Is transport available to Torre d'Águilha?

If required, transport may be available from school to Torre d'Águilha between 4:30 pm and 5:30 pm, with a reference price of +€10 per day. See the transport page.

Where do lessons take place?

At Seminário Torre d'Águilha in São Domingos de Rana, Cascais.
