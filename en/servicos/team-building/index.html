What it is

Music team building: the voice as a team instrument.

Music team building is a team development methodology that uses choral singing and vocal exercises as a vehicle to work on communication, trust, active listening and leadership. Unlike games or physical activities, music places everyone at the same level — regardless of role, department or seniority.

With over 20 years of experience in group formation, VoxLaci sessions are not about music. They are about confronting fears, exploring new resources and building individual and collective confidence in the pursuit of a shared goal. Results are visible within the session itself.

The process

How a VoxLaci session works.

Each session is tailored to the company, but always follows a pedagogical progression tested over two decades.

Step 01

Breathing and presence

The session begins with breathing and awareness exercises. The group relaxes, activates and starts to find a common rhythm — before any music.

Step 02

Voice and expression

Progressive vocal exercises: discovering what each participant has, without judgement. The comfort zone starts to expand.

Step 03

Singing together

The group sings together for the first time. Each voice has a role. The group learns to listen to others while singing — the essence of teamwork.

Step 04

Performance and reflection

The session culminates in a collective performance. The group reviews what it experienced and transfers those lessons to the professional context.

Why music

Three reasons why choir team building works for companies.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about music team building?

What is music team building?

Music team building is a team development methodology that uses choral singing and vocal exercises to work on communication, trust, active listening and leadership. Unlike games or physical activities, music places everyone at the same level — regardless of role — with results visible within the session itself.

Do participants need musical experience?

No. No musical experience is required. The sessions are designed for any professional profile, regardless of musical background. As Myguel Santos e Castro says: "If you have a voice to speak, you have a voice to sing."

How many participants can a session accommodate?

VoxLaci sessions work with groups of 7 to 700 participants. The dynamics and format adapt to team size — a session for 15 people has a different approach to one for 200.

Are the results immediate?

Yes. Results are visible within the session. Participants report feeling more positive about their place in the company and their relationship with colleagues. The effects — energy, joy, sense of belonging — carry over to the workplace in the days and weeks that follow.

How long does a session last?

The base format is 90 minutes. Half-day, full-day and two-day programmes are also available. There is also an Event Opening format (30 to 60 min) for conventions and conferences. The programme adapts to the company's needs and availability.

What is the investment?

The base price is €1,250 for up to 30 participants for 90 minutes (VAT exempt). Each additional 30 minutes costs €250. Discounts of 10% above €3,000 and 20% above €4,000. For larger groups or bespoke programmes, contact us for a personalised quote.

Can sessions take place at our premises?

Yes. Sessions take place at the client's premises or at any chosen location. VoxLaci is based at Seminário Torre d'Águilha in Cascais, 30 minutes from Lisbon — and travels anywhere in Portugal. Travel costs outside Greater Lisbon apply.

Can sessions be conducted in English?

Yes. Myguel Santos e Castro conducts sessions in both Portuguese and English. For multinational companies, international teams or events with participants from different countries, sessions can be entirely in English or in a bilingual format.

What makes choir team building different from other activities?

Music places everyone at the same level, regardless of title. The experience is not about singing well or being in tune — it is about confronting fears, exploring new resources and building collective trust in a context that no other activity can replicate. With over 20 years of experience, VoxLaci delivers an exciting, challenging and professional environment.

Can sessions be filmed or photographed?

Yes. Sessions can be filmed and photographed by the client for use as internal communication content, social media posts or press materials. A team singing together is always powerful, authentic content.
